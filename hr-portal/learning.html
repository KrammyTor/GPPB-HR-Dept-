Official Learning Platform

ProHub: Procurement Professionals Online Hub

ProHub is the official and centralized digital learning platform of the GPPB-TSO. It provides access to training materials, recordings, and e-learning resources for procurement professionals.

On-Demand Modules Access training materials, recordings, and e-learning resources anytime.
Lifetime Access Accredited trainings include continued access to learning resources.
Course Categories Includes Basic Procurement Courses, Specialized Training, and NGPA-specific workshops.
📚
Training Programs

Capacity Development Activities & Training

  • NGPA Caravan & Workshops: The 2026 Q2 trainings focus on readiness for the New Government Procurement Act (RA 12009).
  • Professionalization Calendar: Provides the official schedule of accredited training programs, certification sessions, and academic offerings.
  • Recognized Trainers: Training is facilitated by over 150 GPPB-recognized trainers.
  • Mentorship & Best Practices: Covers data-driven procurement, risk-proofing, and ethical sourcing.
🎓
Certification

Certification and Professionalization Framework

  • NGPA Compliance: The new law mandates a competency framework to standardize procurement positions.
  • Skills Development: Ensures procurement practitioners have the required knowledge and competencies.
  • Professionalization Roadmap: Launched in March 2026 to certify procurement professionals.
  • Career Growth: Supports public trust, professional standards, and long-term career development.
🌐
Digital Services

Digitalization of Training Services

  • Online Training Request System: Since 2023, training requests are managed online.
  • Tracking and Verification: Training tracking and certification verification are handled digitally.
  • Email-Based Requests Ended: The online system replaced the previous email-based process.
  • ProHub App/Platform: Provides mobile-friendly access to learning resources.
🚀
2025–2026 Initiatives

Key Initiatives & Recent Themes

  • Green Public Procurement: Training focused on pilot implementation and sustainable procurement practices.
  • ProcureQuest: An interactive challenge focused on NGPA compliance.
  • In-House Training: Specialized trainings tailored for agencies.
  • Early Procurement Planning: A common focus area for agency-specific training programs.
