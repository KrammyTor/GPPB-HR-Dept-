Featured Achievement

Enactment of the New Government Procurement Act (RA 12009)

The GPPB-TSO spearheaded the development of the New Government Procurement Act (NGPA), which was signed in July 2024 and took effect in August 2024. Its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) followed in February 2025, marking a major reform milestone in the Philippine procurement system.

Digitalization & Innovation

Digital Transformation

  • Launched the Procurement Observer's Portal (POP) on 12 August 2024.
  • Enabled civil society organizations (CSOs) to monitor biddings and submit reports online.
  • Strengthened digital platforms to support the transition to the NGPA.
Efficiency & Accountability

Operational Excellence

  • Received an UNMODIFIED OPINION from the Commission on Audit (COA) for FY 2020, 2021, and 2022.
  • Demonstrated clean, transparent, and accountable financial management.
  • Successfully obtained ISO 9001:2015 Certification for its quality management system.
Capacity Development

Professionalization & Learning

  • Expanded and retooled a network of GPPB-recognized trainers nationwide.
  • Built a more sustainable procurement learning ecosystem.
  • Launched the Procurement Professionalization Roadmap (2026).
Policy Reform

System Strengthening

  • Conducted a comprehensive assessment of the Philippine procurement system using the updated 2018 MAPS methodology.
  • Championed the Green Public Procurement (GPP) Roadmap.
  • Integrated environmental considerations into procurement procedures.
Recognition

Partnerships & Awards

  • Received awards from the Career Executive Service Board (CESB).
  • Recognized by the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA).
  • Received recognition from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP).
Nationwide Reach

NGPA Caravan

  • Established the NGPA Caravan.
  • Trained procurement practitioners across all regions on the new procurement law.
  • Supported awareness, compliance, and implementation nationwide.
