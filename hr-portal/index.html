GPPB-TSO Quality Policy

 

We, at the Government Procurement Policy Board - Technical Support Office, commit to foster a dynamic, innovative and sustainable public procurement system, with mechanisms and policies to support the transformation of government procurement that is responsive to the needs and expectations of its stakeholders.

 

We endeavor to comply with the legal, regulatory and other applicable requirements in providing legal research and advisory services; capacity development; and procurement compliance monitoring of procuring entities towards a transparent, accountable, sustainable and inclusive public procurement regime.

 

We commit to the continual improvement of our Quality Management System by maintaining a highly specialized and exemplary workforce, regular monitoring of quality objectives, assessing the processes' performance and service conformity, and ensuring periodic review of risk management and controls.
